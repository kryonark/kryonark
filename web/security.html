Principles

Your account, your keys

Only your address can withdraw your collateral, borrow, or take back your buffer.

Least authority

The agent can call one function: move your own buffer into your own collateral.

No bad-data actions

A stale or missing price stops borrowing and liquidations instead of acting on it.

You can always leave

Pausing blocks new deposits and borrowing only. Repaying and withdrawing always work.

Guardian agent

The agent canAllowed
Move your ETH buffer into your collateral, while health is below 1.30Yes, until the expiry you set
Withdraw collateral or buffer, to anyoneNo
Borrow, repay, or touch other accountsNo
Act after you revoke it or after it expiresNo (max 90 days, revoke anytime)

If the agent's server or key were compromised, the worst it could do is move your buffer into your collateral early. That's still your ETH, in your account.

Check it in the deployed code

The vault's source is verified on Sourcify (exact match: the bytecode on chain compiles from this file). guardTopUp is the only function that reads guardians, and this is all of it (KryonarkVault.sol, lines 292–306):

function guardTopUp(address account, uint256 amount) external nonReentrant {
    Guardian memory g = guardians[account];
    if (g.agent == address(0) || msg.sender != g.agent) revert NotGuardian();
    if (block.timestamp > g.expiry) revert Expired();
    accrue();
    uint256 before = healthOf(account);
    if (before >= GUARD_HEALTH) revert Healthy(before);
    if (amount == 0 || amount > buffer[account]) revert Amount();
    buffer[account] -= amount;
    totalBuffer -= amount;
    collateral[account][ETH] += amount;
    assets[ETH].total += amount;
    emit Deposited(account, ETH, amount);
    emit GuardTopUp(account, msg.sender, amount, before, healthOf(account));
}

It never sends a token or ETH anywhere: it only moves an amount from buffer[account] to collateral[account][ETH] inside the vault. Every function that sends funds out (withdraw, withdrawBuffer, borrow, unsupply) acts on msg.sender's own account and pays msg.sender; liquidate needs health below 1.00, which anyone can call, guardian or not.

Prices and liquidation

  • Mainnet: ETH and seven stocks (NVDA, AAPL, TSLA, AMZN, MSFT, GOOGL, META) come from Chainlink feeds on Robinhood Chain, refused when older than their heartbeat. Chainlink has no feed for Anthropic (ANT) or USDG yet, so the agent pushes those: ANT from Robinhood's token quote, USDG at $1.00. Each push can move a price at most 25%, prices older than an hour are refused, and the owner can switch an asset to a Chainlink feed once one exists.
  • Testnet: Chainlink has no feeds there, so the agent pushes ETH (Coinbase) and Stock Token (Robinhood) prices. Each push can move a price at most 25%, and prices older than an hour are refused.
  • Liquidation needs health below 1.00. Liquidators repay at most half the debt and receive 5% extra collateral.
  • Stock markets close and crypto doesn't: weekend gaps are why borrowing stops at 1.25 and the agent steps in at 1.30.

What the owner can do

The owner key (the team's deployer today; a multisig with a timelock before meaningful deposits) can:

  • list assets and set their weight (max 90%) and deposit caps; set the borrow rate (max 50% a year) and the borrow cap;
  • pause new deposits, borrowing and supplying (never repaying, withdrawing or exiting);
  • on the oracle: choose each asset's price feed and, for assets without a feed, set a price directly.

The oracle powers are the most sensitive: a wrong feed or price could trigger liquidations. That's why the owner key moves to a multisig with a timelock, and why every change is an on-chain event anyone can watch. The owner can't withdraw or move anyone's collateral, buffer or deposits.

Risks

  • Not audited yet. The contracts are open source with tests, but no external audit has been done. Only deposit what you can afford to lose.
  • Stock Tokens are controlled by Robinhood. Their contracts can pause transfers, block addresses, and burn from any address (admin burn). If the vault were blocked or a token paused, that token couldn't move until it's lifted.
  • Not for US persons. Robinhood Stock Tokens may not be offered, sold or delivered in the United States or to US persons.
  • Market risk. Prices can gap, especially over weekends; a large enough move liquidates part of your collateral.
  • Dependencies. Robinhood Chain, Chainlink, the stablecoin issuer and your wallet are outside Kryonark's control.
