Security

The agent can add.
It can't withdraw.

Kryonark is built so that no single part can take your collateral: it stays on its home chain, the AI agent signs with a key that can only top up, and every price and every action is public on-chain.

Principles

Collateral stays home

SOL stays on Solana, BTC on Bitcoin, USDC on Base. Nothing is bridged by hand.

Least authority

The agent's key can add collateral and nothing else. It can't withdraw, trade or move funds out.

Public prices

Health factors use Chainlink feeds for both the collateral and the stock, the same numbers anyone can check.

You can always leave

Revoke the agent at any time. Closing a position repays the stock side and unlocks your collateral on its home chain.

Collateral custody

When you open a position, your collateral is locked in a vault contract on the chain where it already lives. A cross-chain message tells Robinhood Chain that the collateral is locked; only then is the stock position opened there.

  • The vault on the home chain only releases collateral when Robinhood Chain confirms the position is repaid or closed.
  • No wrapped copy of your asset is minted, so there is no bridge pool holding everyone's funds.
  • Liquidations, if they ever happen, follow the same path in reverse and are visible on both chains.

Agent session key

The AI agent never holds your main wallet key. You grant it a session key (ERC-7715 style permissions on an ERC-4337 account) with a narrow scope:

PermissionAllowed
Add collateral to your own positionYes, from the buffer you set
Sweep yield into your bufferOnly if you enable it
Withdraw to any addressNo
Open, close or trade positionsNo, only you can
Exceed the buffer or the time limitNo (default 30 days, revoke anytime)

If the agent, its server or its model were compromised, the worst it could do is move your own buffer into your own position early.

Prices and liquidation

Health = collateral value × 0.8 ÷ position value. Below 1.00 a position can be liquidated. The agent steps in below 1.30, long before that.

  • Both sides are priced from Chainlink feeds on Robinhood Chain; stale or out-of-range prices pause new positions instead of liquidating on bad data.
  • Robinhood Chain blocks are about 100 ms, so the agent can react within the same minute as a market move.
  • Stock markets close; crypto doesn't. Weekend and overnight gaps are why the buffer and the guard threshold exist.

Cross-chain messaging

Lock and unlock instructions travel through established messaging and intent layers (LayerZero V2, Across) rather than a custom bridge. Each message is verified on the destination chain before the vault acts on it.

Risk that remains: Kryonark depends on those layers and on the chains themselves. That risk is listed here rather than hidden.

This website

  • kryonark.xyz never asks for a seed phrase, a private key or a signature, and no one from Kryonark will ever ask for them.
  • The engine on the home page is a preview: an example book with live collateral prices. It doesn't connect to a wallet or send transactions.
  • The site is static, served over HTTPS, and its full source is public at github.com/kryonark/kryonark.
  • The only official links are kryonark.xyz, github.com/kryonark and the kryonark npm package.
